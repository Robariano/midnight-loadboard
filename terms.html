Plain-language terms for using Midnight Loadboard.

Last updated: July 2026

What Midnight Loadboard Is

Midnight Loadboard is a listing platform. We connect shippers who have freight with carriers who have trucks. We are not a broker, we are not a party to the transaction between a shipper and a carrier, and we do not process, guarantee, or hold payment for any load.

Once a load is claimed, the shipper and carrier are responsible for finalizing pickup, delivery, rate, and payment directly with each other.

Carrier Verification

We review documents submitted by carriers — CDL, insurance, and FMCSA operating authority — before listing them as "verified." Verification means we reviewed what was submitted and it appeared legitimate at the time; it is not a guarantee of a carrier's ongoing insurance status, safety record, or performance on any given load. Shippers should independently confirm a carrier's current authority and insurance before dispatching high-value freight, and are encouraged to use the FMCSA SAFER system directly for the latest record.

No Guarantee of Payment or Performance

Midnight Loadboard does not guarantee that a shipper will pay a carrier, that a carrier will complete a delivery, or that any specific payment term (including "Net 7") will be honored. These are agreements made directly between the shipper and carrier. If a payment or performance dispute arises, it is between those two parties.

Revoked Credentials

If we later learn that a carrier provided false information or acted fraudulently, we'll list them on the Revoked Credentials page. This is informational only — it does not undo any transaction that already occurred, and we are not liable for transactions completed before a revocation was posted.

Acceptable Use

  • Don't submit false or fraudulent CDL, insurance, or authority documents.
  • Don't post fake loads or use the claim system to spam carriers.
  • Don't scrape or bulk-collect data from the site.

We reserve the right to remove listings, revoke a carrier's verified status, or deny access to anyone violating these terms.

This is an early-stage platform and these terms may change as it develops. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated terms.
