About the Founder

Built From the Inside.

I didn't build this because I read about trucking. I built it because I've been in it — and I got tired of watching the same problems never get fixed.

Where I Come From

My family has run A&L Coors, a beer distribution business based in Durango, Colorado, since 1933. I grew up around it — delivery routes, warehouse work, loading docks. I held my own routes, left, came back, left again. That's the nature of the business.

I hold a CDL with all endorsements. I'm a Navy veteran. One of my assignments in the Navy was truck inspection — unloading civilian flatbeds, blocking and bracing cargo, inspecting loads moving between a weapons station and naval vessels. I know what it actually takes to move freight safely and what real documentation looks like.

What I Kept Running Into

Every time I was driving or working around freight, the same things kept coming up. Routing tools that weren't built for trucks. Brokers who were slow to pay or hard to reach after the load was delivered. Carriers who couldn't prove their credentials fast enough to get work. Paperwork that got lost and became a dispute.

None of these are new problems. They've been around forever. What bothered me was that nobody was building something that actually addressed them from the carrier's side.

Why This Exists

Freight has a trust problem. Brokers don't know who they're actually hiring. Carriers can't prove who they are without jumping through endless hoops. Payment drags out 30, 60, 90 days. The whole system runs on phone calls and people hoping the other side is telling the truth.

Midnight Loadboard is built on a simple idea: verify every carrier personally before they touch a load, put the payment terms upfront, and don't make anyone wait 30 days to get paid for work they already did. Shippers know who they're hiring. Carriers get paid in 7 days. No factoring companies, no broker games.

I built this myself. If you're a carrier looking for real loads or a shipper who wants verified drivers, get verified or post a load.
