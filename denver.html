Small Trucks, Big Hub

Why hotshots and cargo vans fit Denver

Not every Colorado load needs a 53-foot trailer. A lot of Front Range freight is urgent, partial, or headed somewhere a big rig can't get to quickly. That's work sized for hotshots and cargo vans.

HOTSHOT

Pickup + flatbed, moving now

Oil and gas fields in Weld County, construction and equipment work along the Front Range, and mountain towns west on I-70 all need parts, pipe, tools and materials moved fast.

  • Time-critical partial flatbed loads
  • Equipment and parts runs to job sites
  • Mountain deliveries where a smaller rig is faster
CARGO VAN

Expedited and partial freight

Denver's warehouses and distribution centers ship plenty of freight that fills a few pallets, not a trailer: parts, medical supplies, e-commerce, and anything that has to go today.

  • Same-day and next-day runs across the region
  • 1–4 pallet loads that don't justify a semi
  • Long runs out of Denver to anywhere in the lower 48
