Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Last updated: July 2026

What We Collect

Depending on which form you use, we collect: your name, email, and phone number; for carriers, your CDL class, DOT/MC number, and links to your CDL, insurance certificate, and FMCSA operating authority documents that you provide; for shippers, load details like pickup/delivery locations, dates, and rates.

We do not ask for or store payment information, bank details, or Social Security numbers. We don't collect location data, and we don't use tracking cookies or analytics scripts.

How We Use It

  • To verify carrier credentials (CDL, insurance, operating authority) before listing them as a verified carrier.
  • To list your load or carrier profile on the board so the other side of the marketplace can see and act on it.
  • To contact you about your submission — approval status, questions, or to facilitate a match.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties except as described below.

Where It's Stored

Submissions are stored in a Supabase database. Document links you provide (CDL, insurance, authority) point to files you host yourself (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.) — we don't host or copy those files ourselves, we just store the link.

If you use the "Check FMCSA" lookup on a verification, we query the federal FMCSA QCMobile public database using your submitted DOT/MC number to check operating authority status — no additional data is sent to FMCSA beyond that number.

Who Can See It

Load requests and carrier verifications are reviewed by the site admin before anything is made public. If a carrier is approved, their name and basic info appear on the public Carriers page. Loads you post appear on the public Browse Loads page once published — this includes pickup/delivery, equipment, and rate, but not your personal contact info, which is shared directly with a carrier once they claim it.

Your Choices

You can ask us to delete your submitted information at any time by emailing robertariano@gmail.com. If you're an approved carrier and want to be removed from the public Carriers page, the same applies.

Changes

This is a small, early-stage platform and this policy may change as the product grows. We'll update the date above when it does.
